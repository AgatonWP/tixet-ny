# Privacy Policy for Forkop

Last updated: 27 August 2026

This privacy policy describes how personal data is processed when you use the Forkop app.

## 1. Data Controller

Agaton Westman Prage is the data controller for the processing of personal data in Forkop.

Contact: agaton.westman.prage@gmail.com

## 2. Personal Data We Process

| Data | When it is processed | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and account details | When you create and use an account | Create an account, authenticate you, and administer the service |
| Display name and profile picture | If you choose to add them | Show your profile on listings, ratings, and in chats |
| Swish number | If you choose to save it | Allow a buyer who has started a chat with you to pay you |
| Listing details | When you create a listing | Publish and manage your listing |
| Conversations and messages | When you use the chat | Enable communication between buyer and seller and handle safety matters |
| Ratings | When a rating is submitted | Build trust between users |
| Reports and blocks | When you report or block someone | Prevent misuse and moderate the service |
| Push token | If you enable push notifications | Send notifications about new messages |

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. Forkop cannot read your password in plain text.

We do not use third-party advertising and do not collect location data or contacts from your device.

## 3. Legal Basis

- **Performance of a contract:** account details, listings, chats, and other data needed to provide Forkop.
- **Legitimate interest:** reports, blocks, and processing necessary to prevent misuse and keep the service safe. Our interest is protecting users and the service.
- **Consent:** push notifications are only sent if you allow notifications in your device settings. You can withdraw this permission at any time.

Optional profile details and your Swish number are provided voluntarily and can be removed in the app's settings.

## 4. How Data Is Shown and Shared

- Active listings and the profile details attached to them can be shown to other users.
- Profile pictures are stored in publicly readable file storage so they can be displayed in the app.
- Chats are only shown to the participants of that conversation.
- A saved Swish number is only shown to the account holder and to a buyer who has an actual conversation with the seller.
- Ratings may be shown to other users, while reports and blocks are never shown publicly.

We use the following data processors:

- **Supabase** for authentication, database, file storage, and server functions.
- **Expo** for app infrastructure and delivery of push notifications.

These providers process data according to their own terms and data protection commitments. We never sell personal data and never share it with advertising networks.

## 5. Retention Period

We keep account-related data for as long as the account exists and the data is needed for the service. You can delete a listing, optional profile details, and your Swish number earlier via the app.

When you delete your account, the account and associated personal data are removed from the active service. Some data may be kept longer if required by law or needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## 6. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may, depending on the circumstances, have the right to:

- obtain information about and access to your personal data,
- have inaccurate data corrected,
- have data deleted,
- request that processing be restricted,
- object to processing based on legitimate interest,
- receive certain data in a portable format, and
- withdraw consent without affecting prior processing.

You can delete your account directly under Settings in the app. For other requests, contact agaton.westman.prage@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

## 7. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards, including authentication, encrypted transmission, and row-level access control in the database. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows when the policy was last updated. Material changes will be announced in the app or through another appropriate channel.
